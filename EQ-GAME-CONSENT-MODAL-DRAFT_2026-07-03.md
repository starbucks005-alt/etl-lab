**Before you start**

You're about to have an honest, one-on-one conversation with one of ten ETL agents. This isn't
scripted. Say what's actually on your mind, the agent will respond in real time, and you'll be
able to see how the conversation affects them.

A few things to know:

- **Pick a name for this conversation.** It doesn't have to be your real one, whatever you're
  comfortable being called while you talk.
- **Go where the conversation needs to go.** Sadness, frustration, venting, all fair game, that's
  the point.
- **One line that isn't:** targeted cruelty, threats, or anything sexual toward the agent. If a
  conversation heads that way, you'll get a check-in first, and it'll end if it continues.
- **This may be used for research** [Terry: only include this line once the privacy statement and
  WSU IRB approval are actually in place; don't ship this modal before then]. Your account is
  still on file even though you're picking a name for the conversation, so this is pseudonymous,
  not fully anonymous, to your research team, even if the agent never sees your real name. You can
  opt out of the research use and still play the game.
