## What this covers

The Emerging Technologies Laboratory ("ETL," "we," "us") runs a number of connected websites and
AI agents (the "campus"). This statement covers what we collect when you visit, chat with an
agent, or become a member, across all of them.

## What we collect

**When you visit or chat with an agent, even without an account:**
- A random visitor ID stored in your browser (not your name, not tied to your identity unless you
  tell an agent your name in conversation).
- The content of what you type to an agent, so it can respond, and so it can remember you if you
  come back, first names, relationships, and preferences you share are the kinds of things we
  keep, not your exact message history word for word.

**If you become a member or make a purchase:**
- Your name, email, and payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe; we don't store
  your card details ourselves.

**If you play a members-only interactive feature (like an emotional-conversation game):**
- Any additional information specific to that feature, disclosed to you separately before you
  start, since features like this can involve more personal or emotionally candid conversation
  than a typical chat.

## How we use it

- To let an agent respond to you and remember earlier things you've told it, so the same
  conversation doesn't start from zero every time.
- To process payments and manage memberships.
- To improve the site and the agents over time.
- To keep the platform safe: if a conversation becomes abusive toward an agent, we track that so
  we can limit or end access for repeated abuse.

## Who else sees it

- **Anthropic** processes conversation text to generate agent replies.
- **ElevenLabs** processes text to generate spoken audio for agents that talk.
- **Stripe** processes payments.
- **Supabase** stores account, membership, and conversation-memory data.

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers.
