AL, companion panel

Meet AL, a companion you can shape

Customize her look, voice, accent, and skills to fit you. Start a conversation or share a photo so she learns who you are.

Interactive preview only. Features, traits, and pricing shown are sample data; no payment is collected.

1. Eye color

Her eyes are full-color lights. Teal is her default.

Brightness is capped to protect the power supply.

2. Accent

Pick how she sounds, then press Hear it.

3. Name

A companion's name can be changed. AL's is locked.

4. Skills

Download new abilities. These are examples with example prices, not for sale.

5. Personalities

Not everyone wants the same companion. Download a different personality for AL: these are characters from The Dose, The Gym, Almost Human and Good Company. Press Hear voice to listen first. She is still AL, and the prices are examples, with no payment taken.

6. Talk to AL

Type a message. In demo mode the replies are examples.

7. Show her a photo

Share a photo so AL gets to know you. She tells you what she sees.

Only share photos you are happy for AL to see. When AL is connected, the photo is sent to her AI service to be described.
Concept and prototype stage. AL is a physical companion from Emerging Technologies Laboratory. She is an android, not a person.
