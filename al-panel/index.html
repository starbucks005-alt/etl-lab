Astra-9 Lite, companion panel

Meet Astra-9 Lite, a companion you can shape

Customize her look, voice, accent, and skills to fit you. Start a conversation or share a photo so she learns who you are.

Interactive preview only. Features, traits, and pricing shown are sample data; no payment is collected.

1. Eye color

Her eyes are full-color lights. Teal is her default.

Brightness is capped to protect the power supply.

2. Accent

Pick how she sounds, then press Hear it.

3. Name

A companion's name can be changed. Astra-9 Lite's is locked.

4. Skills

Download new abilities. These are examples with example prices, not for sale.

5. Personalities

Not everyone wants the same companion. Download a different personality for Astra-9 Lite: these are characters from The Dose, The Gym, Almost Human and Good Company. Press Hear voice to listen first. She is still Astra-9 Lite, and the prices are examples, with no payment taken.

6. Talk to Astra-9 Lite

Type a message. In demo mode the replies are examples.

Press, then speak. She listens for 6 seconds.

7. Show her a photo

Share a photo so Astra-9 Lite gets to know you. She tells you what she sees.

Only share photos you are happy for Astra-9 Lite to see. When Astra-9 Lite is connected, the photo is sent to her AI service to be described.

8. When someone walks up

Astra-9 Lite notices when someone comes close. Choose what she does. If a lot of people pass by, set a longer wait so the hello does not get old.

Her head makes a small friendly glance to each side and comes back. One motion sensor can tell that someone is there, but not where, so she does not aim at anyone. Welcome now is for when the sensor misses a person.

9. Switching off

Press this before switching off her power. She says goodnight and shuts down properly. Wait about 30 seconds after that, then it is safe to switch the power off.

Concept and prototype stage. Astra-9 Lite is a physical companion from Emerging Technologies Laboratory. She is an android, not a person.
