      Show her a photo or a file

      Show her a photo so she gets to know you, or give her a file to read. She tells you what she sees, or what stands out in it.

      Only share photos and files you are happy for A.L.I.C.E. to see. When she is connected, the photo or file is sent to her AI service to be read, and is not saved. She keeps the first part of a file in mind for the next few questions.

              People she knows about

              She cannot tell who is speaking, because she does not recognize voices or faces. But she can know about the people you tell her about, and she will take someone's word for who they are. Write whatever you want her to know, one thought per line, in plain sentences. A line like Name | who they are works too. Only Alice gets this, never the characters, and it stays on this robot.

              Voices she knows

              She can learn to tell registered voices apart, but only for people who agree. A person agrees by saying so out loud while she listens. She keeps a fingerprint of the voice, which is a list of numbers, and never the recording. It only helps her choose a greeting. It is not a lock, because a recording of someone could fool it, so keep anything important behind the access code. Everyone else is still a visitor, and she does not guess.

              Add a voice. The person says: I agree that this robot may remember my voice. and then keeps talking for about ten seconds. A second recording makes it surer.

              Pet companion

              While you are out she says a few gentle words to your pet now and then, with her eyes steady and her head still. She does not listen for barking yet, and she makes no claim about how your pet feels. If your pet seems worried or unwell, ask a vet or a trainer. Keep her and her cords out of your pet's reach.

              Her calendar: read only

              Paste the link from Outlook (Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, Publish a calendar, can view all details, then copy the ICS link). She can read what is on it and tell you your day. She cannot add, change or delete anything. Anyone who has the link can read your calendar, so do not share it. It is kept only on this robot and never shown again. A repeating event whose single days were moved or cancelled may be wrong, so check anything important.

              House controls: lights, plugs and blinds

              An option. She does nothing here until you add a device, and she never touches Alexa or anything you already have. She will not control locks, doors, garage doors, alarms, cameras or heaters. Add a device, press Test, and then say "turn on the test lamp." The device must be on the same network as she is.

                For a Kasa plug she needs one small add-on on the Pi: pip3 install python-kasa. Find the plug's address in your router's list of devices.

                When someone walks up

                A.L.I.C.E. notices when someone comes close. Choose what she does. If a lot of people pass by, set a longer wait so the hello does not get old.

                After the hello she listens for about 20 seconds and answers what is said, and keeps going while people keep talking. Always listening uses some speech service credit each time anyone speaks nearby, so switch it off when it is not needed. Only on A.L.I.C.E.

                Event: a hello for each visitor, then she waits to be asked. Lab: she listens all the time and answers her name.
                Her head makes a small friendly glance to each side and comes back. One motion sensor can tell that someone is there, but not where, so she does not aim at anyone. Welcome now is for when the sensor misses a person.

                Switching off

                Press this before switching off her power. She says goodnight and shuts down properly. Wait about 30 seconds after that, then it is safe to switch the power off.
