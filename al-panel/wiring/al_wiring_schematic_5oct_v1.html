For Astra-9 and Astrad

  • The HAT sockets are 2.54 mm JST XH, 4 pin. Measure each plug before connecting anything: the red wire on the GPIO12 plug is 3.3 V and stays unused.
  • Do not assume the motor is on channel 0. Try each channel in turn with the motor connected and note where it moves.
  • Keep the Pi's built-in audio on. Her voice plays by card number, and switching it off silenced her.
  • Power the Pi first, then the 5 V strip, and shut down with the panel button before cutting power.
  • A buzz in the speakers followed the eye brightness and travelled along the audio cable. Its cause is not fully known. A ground loop isolator on the audio cable is the next thing to try, and it has not been tried yet.
  • Thin jumper wires and twisted joints came loose or frayed at the speakers and plugs. Use screw connectors or solder, support each wire, and leave a little slack.
  • Put a close-up photo of every board beside this page, with a tape flag on each wire, so the dashed lines can be made solid.
