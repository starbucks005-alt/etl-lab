2. What the Service Is

Almost Human allows you to have a text-based conversation with one or more AI-driven "agents," fictional personas with generated backstories and a real-time, instrumented emotional-state display. You may optionally provide a display name and enable a "remember me" feature, which allows the Service to recall details from prior conversations tied to your browser or device. One-on-one conversation with a single agent is free, subject to a daily message limit. A paid tier is available on a monthly subscription basis; it removes the daily limit and unlocks group conversation ("table chat") with multiple agents at once, drawing on a monthly allotment of messages that can be supplemented with additional one-time purchases. Group conversation is only available on the paid tier.
