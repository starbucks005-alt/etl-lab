It started with Rosie. A robot who belonged in the family, not just useful in it. Astra-9 and Astrad are our answer to that.

Meet the newest members of your family, Astra-9 and her companion, Astrad.

Physical robot companions, built for your home.

Working prototype, in testing

Astra-9 and Astrad bring conversational intelligence into the physical room. They sense when you walk in, turn to look at you, and talk back in their own natural voices.

Built on operational behavioral frameworks from Emerging Technologies Laboratory, they carry memory, track context, and adapt their tone over time. They hold an actual conversation instead of reciting canned voice-assistant commands.

Astra-9 is the ninth of nine, the number is what tells her apart from the eight sisters before her who never woke up. Astrad was built after her, for her, so she would finally have the company she'd waited for.

The images below show our current prototype in development, including Astra-9 Lite, the portable version: a head and bust running Astra-9's own real voice and personality. She travels, takes up less room, and should cost less than a full-size figure, so she suits a school, a desk, or a workshop that wants a companion without a whole body. Both sizes arrive ready to talk, expanding through downloadable skill backpacks configured for your home, classroom, or workshop. A cast of characters

Science mode

For people who check what she says. She looks things up in real journals, shows her sources, and says so when she does not know, instead of making something up.

Modes for the moment

Personal assistant

Your calendar, read only

Paste your published calendar link once and she can tell you what is on today, tomorrow or this week, and your next meeting. She cannot add, change or delete anything.

Built, being tried

Bench Guide

Give her a protocol or a recipe and she reads it one step at a time, waits for you to say next, and offers a timer when a step has a wait. Good for hands that are busy.

Built, being tried

Knowledge Shelf

Give her your handbook or your rules as text files. She answers only from them, says where she found it, and says so when it is not there.

Built, being tried

Keeps your pet company

While you are out she says a few gentle words to your pet by name, and mentions a favorite toy now and then, with her eyes steady and her head still. She sends you a daily note of how often she spoke. She does not listen for barking yet.

Built, being tried

Children's friend

Gentle, with characters and skills chosen for anyone under 13, and notes a parent can read. She never pretends to be a person, a pet or a family member.

Built, being tried

A front-door greeter

Welcomes visitors, asks where they are going, gives directions from your own building directory, points to forms, and tells the front office who is here. In an emergency she says call 911 first. A preview of the ETL Guardians.

Built, being tried

Lights, plugs and blinds

An option you add. Say "turn on the lamp" or "close the blinds." She works alongside what you already have and will not touch locks, doors or alarms.
