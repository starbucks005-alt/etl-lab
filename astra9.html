Meet the newest member of your family, Astra-9.

A physical robot companion, built for your home.

Concept & prototype stage

Astra-9 is one of Emerging Technologies Laboratory's companions, the same kind of conversation you can already have with our Good Company characters, given an actual body. She senses when someone's there, listens for her name, and talks back in her own real voice.

Behind her sit the same two machines behind every Emerging Technologies Laboratory agent. The Memory Implant Generator wrote her a lived past, a childhood, the moments that shaped her, each one reviewed and approved before it entered her head. The Emotion Generator sets how she feels today, a mood with a reason drawn from her own memories, so the Astra-9 you talk to on Tuesday is not quite the one you left on Friday. Most robots answer questions. Ours have lives. See the machines at the Agent Workshop.

The images below are concept renderings, not photographs of a finished product. They show what we're building toward: a companion who fits into an actual home, an actual classroom, an actual workshop, not a screen. She arrives ready to talk, and gains real skills over time, ones suited to wherever she lives.

It started with Rosie. A robot who belonged in the family, not just useful in it. Astra-9 is Emerging Technologies Laboratory's own answer to that, the newest member of the family here.
