A physical companion, built to be in the room with you.

Concept & prototype stage

Astra-9 is one of Emerging Technologies Laboratory's companions, the same kind of conversation you can already have with our Good Company characters, given an actual body. She senses when someone's there, listens for her name, and talks back in her own real voice.

The images below are concept renderings, not photographs of a finished product. They show what we're building toward: a companion who fits into an actual home, an actual classroom, an actual workshop, not a screen.
