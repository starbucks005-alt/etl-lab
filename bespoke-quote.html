Why hosting is metered, not flat. Every chat exchange is a live model call and every bio playback is a live voice call; cost tracks real engagement, not time on a shelf. A flat rate either overcharges a small section or quietly loses money on a large one.

Why bespoke work is priced separately from a standard build. A standard agent build (spec, portrait, voice, blink, tool wiring, deployment) is one thing. A shared emotion engine, cross-session memory, multi-agent orchestration, a real instructor syllabus, and custom voice design are systems and specialized work, not agents, and they take real design and testing time beyond any single agent.
