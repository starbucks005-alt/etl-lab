From bottleneck to resolved. Three moves.

About This System

A municipal initiative by the Emerging Technologies Laboratory.

The City Operations Gateway is a pilot architecture built by Dr. Terry L. Oroszi's Emerging Technologies Laboratory in Dayton, Ohio. It applies ETL's AI staffing methodology to municipal operations, demonstrating that city offices can gain on-demand operational capacity without permanent headcount expansion.

Each operator in the system is a role-scoped AI agent: briefed like a staff member, equipped with domain-specific tools, and deployed to a specific function. They do not replace human judgment. They handle the structured, repeatable work that consumes human capacity without requiring human expertise.

This architecture is the foundation for the Delray Beach municipal integration proposal developed in partnership with the Office of the Vice Mayor.
