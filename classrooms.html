How a classroom works

Three steps from concept to a room full of agents ready for your students.

Step 01  ·  You
Fill out the spec

Tell us who the agent is. Their role, their subject matter, their voice, their knowledge domain. For Living Legends, their era, their language, their sources. The spec form takes about 20 minutes per agent.

Step 02  ·  ETL
We build and test

Portrait, blink animation, voice design, backpack wiring, persona refinement. We run the agent through a review before it goes live. Turnaround is typically 3 to 5 business days per agent.

Step 03  ·  Your classroom
Your agents move in

Your department gets a dedicated classroom on the ETL campus. Students access it through a link. No app, no account required on their end. You manage who can talk to which agents. We keep the lights on.
