EQ Room Ratings

Every "End Conversation" exit survey from Almost Human: the visitor's own 1-5 humanness rating next to a model-generated score of the same full conversation, run fresh at the moment the visitor leaves. The finding is the gap between the two, not either number alone — and the model number is a generated output shaped like a self-report, not introspection. Source data for the conference dataset.
