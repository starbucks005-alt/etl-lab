The brief

We read your logo, your colours and your type off your own site. This is also the only link that will appear on the piece; nothing gets invented.
Send a photograph if you have one. A real photo of your product, your room or your people becomes the artwork itself. An upload always wins over the website. Not your logo, that goes below.
Your image goes straight into the piece and Chris draws nothing. Yuki still sets the type and the layout around it.
Goes bottom right on every piece, at the same size, every time.
Hex values, separated by commas. Given these, Yuki builds the palette from them.
Give us neither and Yuki will invent a look from scratch, and pitch you on it at the table.
The more specific you are, the less generic it comes back.
Optional. Pick one and the team builds it. Leave it and Yuki pitches you one.
