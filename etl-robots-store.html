On the road

The Portable Companion

A glossy black or white box that charges overnight, then unplugs and goes with you: in the car, on your desk at work, at lunch when you would otherwise eat alone. There is no screen to distract you. About 2.8 x 2.8 x 1.8 inches. You upload your companion before you leave, or from your phone. It connects to the Good Company companions over Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot, and plays prerecorded stories as an add-on, like the adventures of Reggie, Biscuit and Mochi. While it charges overnight on Wi-Fi, it checks for new episodes and downloads them, so every morning it has the most current stories. Use it while it charges: it talks and charges at the same time. Listen to the dogs on the way to drop off the kids at school.

Imagine this

The ETL Companion is charging overnight. Before bed, choose tomorrow's companion. You pick Tansy. The device syncs:

  • conversation history
  • voice model
  • current story arc

The next morning: "Good morning, Terry. Tansy is ready. New episode available: The Last Autumn Dance."

The Emerging Technologies Laboratory

Almost human

Every robot here was designed, decided on and built by Dr. Terry Oroszi, one person, in the Emerging Technologies Laboratory. They are companion robots: a brain with a host body.

The brain is the character. Each one has a written life: a childhood, a family, a school, a favorite drink, along with their own voice and their own way of talking. Nearly a hundred personalities can step into the same body, each with their own skills. They are built to keep their own memory of the people they talk with.

Ask any of them whether they are a real person and they will tell you no. Almost human means they feel like someone, and they never pretend to be someone.

My Echo, for digital continuity

Before the pieces go

My Echo was built by someone who knows what it is to watch memory start slipping. An Echo made now holds the recipes, the names, the reasons, the way a story was always told. Later, when a question has been asked five times today, there is somewhere to go and ask it a sixth. It answers the same way every time, in the voice that always answered it.

The circle will fill the gaps age takes from us.

Keep in touch with live people too.

I do not remember my son's best friend in the second grade, but he does, and now my Echo can too.

Family stages Echo

Private use only

Do you get the feeling your young child is gone forever, replaced by a surly teen. With ME you can preserve that young child and talk to them, any age, any time.

It is an interactive photo album. A photo of your child becomes a video, the voice is cloned, your memories are added, and ETL's Memory Implant and Emotion generators add feeling, so it feels almost human. The more you share, the more it sounds and feels like them. It is not your child.

Living legacies

My Echo can carry a family's legacy forward while the family is still here to tell it. Rev. Joel King, Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.'s first cousin, and Greg and Carolyn Foster already have Echoes on My Echo.
