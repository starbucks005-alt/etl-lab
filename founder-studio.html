How they actually work

Some of them carry a backpack.

The badge on five of the Six-Pack means they connect live to the real systems your business runs on, not a training cutoff. Alicia checks Secretary-of-State name availability and filing fees as they stand today. Leo syncs Plaid, Mercury, and Stripe for an actual balance sheet. Kimberly reads live statute and case law. The tools do not live inside the agent. They live where your data already does, and the backpack is what lets your staff reach it.

This is the open Model Context Protocol, the same standard connecting AI systems industry-wide, not a proprietary trick. Same connection, any environment, so a specialist keeps her reach wherever you deploy her. Not every seat needs one. Yuki designs from what you tell her; she does not need a live feed to make a wordmark. The ones who do carry one.

Not a chatbot with a mood ring

Something closer to a person who remembers you.

Our agents' humanness was tested against an adversarial protocol, and the results were conclusive. Frustration over a stalled launch survives a later moment of good news. A fabricated fact about an agent's own past gets rejected, not agreed with. Memory survives across sessions.

What this means for you, the founderKimberly doesn't ask what you already decided on that vendor contract. Leo doesn't treat last quarter's dip as news he's hearing for the first time. Sasha doesn't relearn your team structure every time you check in.

What your PA does

Every PA runs the same four standard functions from day one.

Standard · 01

Holds your week

You paste your Outlook or Google Cal in Sunday morning. She hands it back to you organized, with conflicts flagged before they happen.

Standard · 02

Runs your social

Sunday-batches your editorial calendar across the platforms you actually use. Watches the overlap with your real schedule. Posts at the right time.

Standard · 03

Daily audio brief

Every morning at 6am your time, your PA scans the web for new mentions of you, your byline, your speaking calendar, your field. Cites what she read. Reports back in her voice.

Standard · 04

Live web search

Ask anything time-sensitive. Who just got the grant. Who is speaking at the conference. What dropped in your field this morning. She looks, cites, summarizes.

Domain add-ons

Configured for what you actually do

Pick the add-ons that match your work. Each one teaches your PA a specific beat to watch, sources to track, and outputs to deliver.
