What your PA does

Every PA runs the same four standard functions from day one.

Standard · 01

Holds your week

You paste your Outlook or Google Cal in Sunday morning. She hands it back to you organized, with conflicts flagged before they happen.

Standard · 02

Runs your social

Sunday-batches your editorial calendar across the platforms you actually use. Watches the overlap with your real schedule. Posts at the right time.

Standard · 03

Daily audio brief

Every morning at 6am your time, your PA scans the web for new mentions of you, your byline, your speaking calendar, your field. Cites what she read. Reports back in her voice.

Standard · 04

Live web search

Ask anything time-sensitive. Who just got the grant. Who is speaking at the conference. What dropped in your field this morning. She looks, cites, summarizes.

Domain add-ons

Configured for what you actually do

Pick the add-ons that match your work. Each one teaches your PA a specific beat to watch, sources to track, and outputs to deliver.

Author add-on

For writers and book authors

Tracks Amazon and B&N sales rank, royalty statements, new reviews, mentions in press, blurb requests, deadline reminders, manuscript backups, agent and editor correspondence.

Professor add-on

For faculty and PIs

Grant cycle tracking, citation alerts, journal submission status, conference deadlines, course prep reminders, student-supervision rotation, tenure file maintenance.

Business add-on

For executives and operators

Competitive scans, news on key competitors, partnership leads, conference attendee lookups, market intel briefs, hiring signal monitoring.

Stocks add-on

For investors

Portfolio watchlist, market open and close briefs, earnings calendar for held positions, sector rotation news, alerts on flagged tickers, SEC filing summaries.
