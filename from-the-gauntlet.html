What happens next

From pitch to company, in four steps.

  1. Configure your PA. Pick who they are, what they sound like, how they push back. Auggie is the default; you can customize.
  2. Add the 6-pack. Alicia, Leo, Kimberly, Rowan, Yuki, Sasha. They form the day-one company.
  3. Pick two specialists. From the full bench, hire any two who fit your business. Jax for SEO. The Greylander team for authors. Whoever matches your pitch.
  4. Name your company. Your PA will ask, in voice, the first time you log in. You can keep it virtual or, when you are ready, file the LLC.
