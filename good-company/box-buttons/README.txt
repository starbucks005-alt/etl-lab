The five buttons on the hologram box

Left to right: 1 Alice, 2 Reggie, 3 Tansy, 4 Kevin, 5 Terry.

What it does today: a press shows that one hologram on the page (hologram.html already listens for keys 1 to 5).
It shows one at a time. It does not show several at once. If several at once is wanted, that is a bigger change and has not been built.

You can try the page before any parts arrive: open hologram.html on any computer, press Start, then press the keys 1 to 5.

halo_buttons.ino is the board program. It has not been tried on the real board. Parts list and wiring are at the top of that file.
