Questions

Why talk to your AI companion when I can talk to Claude or Gemini for free?
A general assistant remembers facts about you so it can help you better. This does not answer questions. It is somebody: a name, a face, a voice, a life of their own, glad when you walk in rather than useful when you do.
Is this a dating app?
No. This is a companion app focused entirely on friendship. Companions are here to support you and help you engage more with the human world, if you need it.
How do I add scenes (new places to sit with them, images and videos)?
In your companion's room, at the end of the row of scenes, there is a chip that says "+ Add a scene." From there you have two ways in. Describe somewhere you would like them to be, and it gets made for you: a real moving scene, built from the picture you chose when you built them, for $4.99. Or, if you already have something, a video you made elsewhere, or a different photo you would rather they were animated from, bring your own instead, that one is free, wired in right away, no charge at all. Asking costs nothing either way.
What does the Spectate button do?
Some companions have others in their life besides you: Reggie has his dog friends Biscuit and Mochi, Tansy has Poppy and Blue, a household of puppets talks amongst themselves. Wherever that's true, a "Spectate" button shows up in the room. Press it and they carry on their own conversation with each other, a few lines at a time, while you just watch. It winds down on its own after a bit, or stops the moment you type something, or press the button again to end it early. A few scenes even open already mid-conversation, so you can walk in and listen without pressing anything at all.
What if I build a companion and do not like them?
They start as a companion, with the hope of developing a friendship, but that might not happen. If you do not click with your companion, that is okay. You can edit their personality until you do, or start over completely.
How do I get my companion on my phone too?
A companion you build lives in the browser you built them in, not on an account somewhere, so a second device does not just know about them on its own. In their room, look for the "Other device" button next to "Bring someone." Press it there, on the device your companion already lives on, and it gives you a one-time link. Open that link on your phone (or any other device) and they will be there too, exactly as they are. Nothing moves: they stay right where they were as well. The link works once, so make a fresh one if you want to add another device later.
