Questions

Why talk to your AI companion when I can talk to Claude or Gemini for free?
A general assistant remembers facts about you so it can help you better. This does not answer questions. It is somebody: a name, a face, a voice, a life of their own, glad when you walk in rather than useful when you do.
How do I add scenes (new places to sit with them, images and videos)?
In your friend's room, at the end of the row of scenes, there is a chip that says "+ Add a scene." From there you have two ways in. Describe somewhere you would like them to be, and it gets made for you: a real moving scene, built from the picture you chose when you built them, for $4.99. Or, if you already have something, a video you made elsewhere, or a different photo you would rather they were animated from, bring your own instead — that one is free, wired in right away, no charge at all. Asking costs nothing either way.
What does it mean to add my companion to the catalog?
Other people who want a companion but do not want to build one from scratch can browse a catalog of premade companions. Adding yours puts them there. Whoever picks them gets their own copy to start from: the same images, the same scenes, the same personality, and they can rename them and edit any of it from there. What does not come along is any of it that happened between the two of you — no memories, no history, no conversations. They start fresh, the way meeting somebody new always does, just with a face and a personality already in place. You get 200 credits the first time you add a companion to the catalog.
How do I get my companion on my phone too?
A friend you build lives in the browser you built them in, not on an account somewhere, so a second device does not just know about them on its own. In their room, look for the "Other device" button next to "Bring someone." Press it there, on the device your friend already lives on, and it gives you a one-time link. Open that link on your phone (or any other device) and they will be there too, exactly as they are. Nothing moves: they stay right where they were as well. The link works once, so make a fresh one if you want to add another device later.
