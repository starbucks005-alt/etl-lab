Fireside chat

One of them speaks at a time. You hold the floor whenever you want it.

A green dot means that one is switched on and connected. Between chats they check in about every 20 seconds, so a dot can take that long to turn green.

Start with a name to send it only to that one: "Elle, what do you want to learn?" or "ALICE and Elle, are you sisters?". A name at the very end works too: "What is your favorite color, Elle?"
