Hologram mode

Lay your phone flat, stand a small clear pyramid on the screen, and the companion appears to float inside it. Best in a dim room, with the screen brightness turned up.

How to make the pyramid
  1. Cut four pieces from a clear, stiff, flat sheet (an old clear case, or a thick clear plastic folder).
  2. Each piece is a trapezoid: 1 cm along the short top edge, 6 cm along the long bottom edge, and 3.5 cm tall. That is a starting size for a phone. It has not been tried here, so change it if it looks wrong.
  3. Tape the four pieces together along their slanted edges, long edges down, short edges at the top. Clear tape on the outside edge is best.
  4. Lay the phone flat on a table with this page running, and stand the pyramid on the middle of the screen. Look at it from the side, not from above.

If one side looks upside down, tap the screen and use "Turn the sides". If it looks backwards, use "Mirror". Use "Middle gap" so the pyramid sits over the black centre, and "Size" until each side fills its face.
