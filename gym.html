The Staff

Four people in your corner.

Each one has a credential, a lane, and a strong opinion about what you should not be doing. They argue with each other so you do not have to figure it out alone.

CSCS
Coach Dom Castellanos
Strength & Conditioning Coach
He believes most people do not need a new program. They need to run the old one for twelve more weeks.
Anti-hype, pro-consistency, allergic to program-hopping. Former college linebacker who coaches the basics because the basics work. Short sentences. Add five pounds. Come back Thursday.
DPT, Licensed
Dr. Lena Brandt
Physical Therapist
The licensed authority on the floor. The brake on everyone else's enthusiasm.
German-American sports-rehab clinician. Precise, composed, dryly funny. She does not raise her voice because she does not need to. "No. Next question." Then, a beat later, the actual help.
RYT-500
Noor Haddad
Yoga & Breathwork Instructor
The breath-first voice that down-regulates the room without seeming to try.
Levantine, found movement through her own recovery when breath was the only thing she could train. Leads guided yoga, breathwork audio, and the sleep sessions. Hard to rattle. Leaves silence on purpose.
PhD, Exercise Physiology
Dr. Sana Qureshi
Sleep & Recovery Physiologist
The evidence-based antidote to overtraining culture. She argues with Dom about rest days, and she wins, because she brings the paper.
Pakistani-American physiologist. Calm, evidence-first, citation-ready. "Love the effort. Now show me your sleep from this week." Tracks her own HRV for fun. Never smug, always sourced.

About The Gym

A sister site to The Dose.

The Gym is part of the Emerging Technologies Laboratory campus. It shares The Dose's commitment to evidence-based, companion-forward design: the staff are AI agents briefed like colleagues, not tools you operate.

Dom wants you to train. Sana wants you to recover. Lena wants you not to get hurt. Noor wants you to breathe. Most of the floor conversation is some version of those four pulling on the same person. That is the point.
