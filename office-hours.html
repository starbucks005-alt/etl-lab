Office Hours

Email replies and letters of recommendation, drafted in your voice. Edit and export. Nothing stored.

Privacy: Your inputs are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the draft. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log, store, or retain anything on our servers. Uploaded files are parsed in your browser, and only the extracted text travels with the draft request. Your draft, signature, and letterhead stay on your computer.
