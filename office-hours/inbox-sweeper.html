Inbox Sweeper

Paste a batch of student emails. Get a polite tailored reply for each in 30 seconds. Recovers Sunday nights. FERPA-aware.

Privacy: Your inputs are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for drafting. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log, store, or retain anything on our servers. Drafts render in your browser only.
