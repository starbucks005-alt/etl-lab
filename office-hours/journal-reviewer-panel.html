Office Hours · Reviewer Panel

AI-simulated reviewer panel.

Upload your manuscript. Three reviewers (each an AI persona modeled on a distinct academic lens) will read it independently and write their own reports in their own voice. Methodologist, Domain Expert, Skeptic, Theorist, Stats Hardliner, Outside Reader, Interdisciplinary Bridge, Supportive Editor. Editor decision computed from the panel. Built as a pre-submission rigor check, not a replacement for real peer review.

What this is: Each reviewer is an AI persona running on Anthropic’s Claude API. The personas are fictional academic archetypes; the reviews they write are AI-generated. This tool is for pre-submission rigor and rehearsal; journal peer review remains the authoritative process. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log or store anything on our servers. Use a redacted draft if it includes patient identifiers or sensitive collaborators.
