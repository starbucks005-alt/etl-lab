Office Hours · Reviewer Panel

Send your paper to a reviewer panel.

Upload your manuscript. Three reviewers from the ETL faculty roster will read it independently and write their own reports, each in their own voice and lens. The Methodologist, the Domain Expert, the Skeptic, the Theorist, the Stats Hardliner, the Outside Reader. Editor decision computed from the panel.

Privacy: Your paper is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log, store, or retain anything on our servers. Use a redacted draft if it includes patient identifiers or sensitive collaborators.
