Methods Coach

Describe a research design (yours, a colleague's, or a student's). Get strengths, weaknesses, common reviewer criticisms grounded in real literature, defense strategies, and writing guidance.

Privacy: Your inputs are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log, store, or retain anything on our servers. Nothing is saved anywhere after the response comes back.

Analysis
