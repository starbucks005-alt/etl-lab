Paper Reviewer Assistant

A journal asked you to review a paper. Paste it, get a structured draft review with a recommendation, strengths, major and minor concerns, and specific section comments. Edit and submit.

Privacy: Your inputs are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log, store, or retain anything on our servers. Drafts render in your browser only. Confidentiality of unpublished manuscripts remains your responsibility under your journal's reviewer agreement.

Draft review

Edit, restructure, and put it in your own voice before submitting to the journal. Concerns and comments are starting points, not final verdicts.
