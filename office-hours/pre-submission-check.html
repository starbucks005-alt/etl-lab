Office Hours · with Jules

Pre-submission Check.

Editorial last-look on your manuscript before you send it to a journal. Jules reads the draft you already wrote and tells you where reviewers will trip.

This is editorial scrutiny, not AI writing. Paste a manuscript you already wrote. Jules reads it the way a senior colleague would, the night before you submit. She flags reviewer-bait, checks fit against your target journal, looks for missing citations, runs the structural checklist, and drafts a cover letter you can edit. Nothing she returns is meant to replace your prose; everything is meant to make your prose harder to reject.
Privacy: Your manuscript is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log, store, or retain anything on our servers. Nothing is saved after the response comes back. Your unpublished manuscript stays your unpublished manuscript.

Jules's read

Skim, fix what you agree with, ignore what you don't. The cover letter at the bottom is editable in place.
