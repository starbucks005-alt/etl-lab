Office Hours · Resubmission Builder

Turn a Summary Statement into a resubmission.

Paste the reviewer Summary Statement from your rejected federal grant. Get a critique-by-critique response plan in your mechanism's expected convention, a draft Introduction page, and a clear-eyed read on whether the resubmission is winnable. Supports VA, NIH, NSF, DoD CDMRP, AHRQ, HRSA, PCORI, and private foundations.

Read this first: Summary Statements often contain identifiable subject data, pilot study details, or unpublished aims. Your paste is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log, store, or retain anything on our servers. If your statement names patients, sites, or unpublished collaborators, redact those first.
