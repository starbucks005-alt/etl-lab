Office Hours · Reviewer Response

Respond to a journal review.

Reviewer comments came back from a journal. Paste them and the manuscript section under critique. Get a point-by-point response letter in journal convention, revised manuscript text ready to paste back in, and diff notes summarizing what changed.

Privacy: Your reviewer comments and manuscript section are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log or store anything on our servers. This tool is for FACULTY responding to peer review of THEIR OWN manuscripts. For federal grant resubmissions (Summary Statement, Introduction page), use Resubmission Builder instead.

Response package

Letter and revised text are editable in place. Copy individual blocks with the buttons.
