Tenure Dossier Helper

Paste your CV and tell the tool a few things about your institution and your case. Get draft narratives for the teaching, research, and service statements your dossier will require, tuned to your institution's norms.

Privacy: Your CV and inputs are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for drafting. Anthropic does not train on or retain API content. We do not log, store, or retain anything on our servers. Drafts render in your browser only. Your dossier remains your own document.

Draft narratives

Each narrative is editable in place. Click Copy to put it on your clipboard. These are starting points; rewrite in your own voice before submitting to the committee.
