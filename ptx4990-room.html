The Roundtable

Einstein and Curie, at one table.

Pull up a chair. There's tea, if you'd like some.

Say something to the table. They answer you, and they answer each other, the way a real seminar table works, not a queue of one-at-a-time replies. Both scientists are historical figures represented for education, each carrying a real backpack of live sources, Wikipedia and arXiv, so watch for how they cite (or refuse to invent) a source. A cheap "director" model decides who speaks next each beat, favoring genuine back-and-forth over a fixed rotation.
