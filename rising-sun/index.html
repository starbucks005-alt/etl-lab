The Rising Sun

Set the wake time, choose who lives inside the sun, and hear her. Everything here is saved to the sun's account on the website, and the sun reads it from there.

Enter the key to connect

The key

The sun's key, the same one the site and the board hold. It stays in this browser. Without it nothing here can be read or changed.

1. Wake time

The sun is at full strength at this time. It starts coming up before it.

2. Watch the sunrise

A preview of the colors on this screen. Drag to see any moment. The real strip will look different, so tell Claude what to change once it is lit.

3. Who is in the sun

Three fairies come with the sun, each with her own voice. "Use her" puts her in the sun. "Hear her" plays her waking you, using her voice (it needs her voice id and a little ElevenLabs credit). "Change her words" opens her lines below.

Her words and her voice

Anything about her life that is not written in the backstory box, she answers with "I don't know that one". She says she is not a real person if anyone sincerely asks.

4. Text your fairy

Pick who you want to talk to and write to her, the way you would text a friend. She remembers what you told her, so tomorrow she can ask how it went. Each fairy keeps her own conversation. Ask for the news and she plays Above the Fold. What you write is kept on the website behind the key, and "Clear our chat" wipes it.

5. Music

Play Apple Music from here. It plays on the device that has this panel open, such as a tablet beside the sun. The listener signs in with their own Apple Music subscription, and without one only short previews play. Your browser needs a tap first, so music cannot start by itself at wake time.

Save your Apple key under Connections first.

6. Reminders

Write one reminder per line, for example "Take your morning pills." She says them in your words, after her greeting and before she asks about the news. She adds nothing and gives no advice. Up to five. They are kept on the website behind the key.

7. Your calendar

Outlook: Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, Publish a calendar, can view all details, then paste the ICS link. Anyone who has the link can read the calendar, so it is kept on the website and never shown again here. She cannot add, change or delete anything.

The link is saved, but she cannot read it yet. That part is not built. Until it is, she says she cannot see your calendar. The news is Above the Fold from ETL Newswire, the daily briefing, and she plays it when you ask.

Connections

Her thinking runs on Claude through Amazon Bedrock, so the AWS credits pay for it. Paste the Bedrock key here once (from the AWS console: Bedrock, API keys). It is kept on the website and never shown again here. Her voice uses the Azure key the site already has, and the Anthropic key the site already has is the backup. Nothing else to paste.

Apple Music

For the music player below. Three things from your Apple developer account: the Team ID, the Key ID, and the private key file (the .p8). Open the .p8 in a text editor and paste all of it. They are kept on the website and never shown again here. Not shown on screen: the listener signs in with their own Apple Music subscription, so ETL pays nothing for the music.
