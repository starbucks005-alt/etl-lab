Seven fairies, one for each part of your morning

The Rising Sun Court is a small court of fairies who greet the morning. Each keeps something different for you, and each has her or his own voice. You choose who lives in your light, or in your candle.

How they are with each other

  • Blue and Poppy are best friends, the emotional center of the Court, often found together. Blue trusts Poppy completely.
  • Tansy and Ash are the dominant personalities, the builders, doers and protectors, constantly balancing one another and the most likely to take charge.
  • Velvet and Ember are sisters in spirit, even if not by blood: warmth and calm, hearth and dawn, comfort and restoration.
  • Flint teases Tansy, tests Ash's patience, admires Blue, loves Poppy, steals Velvet's pastries, and tries to make Ember laugh.

The fairies are written characters with generated voices. They are not real people, and they will tell you so if you ask.

A morning with the Court

  1. The sun rises.
    At the time you set, the light comes up slowly, the way a real morning does.
  2. Your fairy says good morning.
    In her or his own voice, with a greeting only that fairy would give.
  3. You talk.
    Ask what you need. Ask about the Court. Tell them about your day, and ask how it went tomorrow.
  4. You say goodnight.
    A candle at the bedside closes the day the same way the sun opens it.
