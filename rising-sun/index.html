The seven fairies appear here once you connect with the access code at the top.

Words and voice

Anything about their life that is not written in the backstory box, the fairy answers with "I don't know that one". They say they are not a real person if anyone sincerely asks.

Chat

Write to them the way you would text a friend. They remember what you told them, so tomorrow they can ask how it went. Each fairy keeps a conversation of their own. What you write is kept on the website behind your sun's access code, and "Clear our chat" wipes it.

Reminders

Write one reminder per line, for example "Take your morning pills." The fairy says them in your words, after the greeting and before the question about the news. Nothing is added and no advice is given. Up to five. They are kept on the website behind your sun's access code.

Music

Play Apple Music or Spotify from here. Each listener signs in with their own subscription (Spotify needs Premium). Apple Music plays on the device that has this panel open. Spotify can play on this panel in a computer browser, or this panel can act as a remote for any other Spotify device, such as a phone. Your browser needs a tap first, so music cannot start by itself at wake time.

Little details

Birthdays, favorite songs, the names of the people you love. Write one per line. Start a line with the date as month and day, like 05-14, and your fairy says it on that morning, in their own voice. Lines without a date are things they can bring up when you talk. Up to forty. They are kept on the website behind your sun's access code.

Owner setup (ETL only)

Customers never see this section. ETL holds the provider keys and pays for the thinking, the voice and the music setup. A customer only signs in to their own music account, and later sees their plan and credits. These fields are for ETL while the sun is a prototype.

The fairies' thinking runs on Claude through Amazon Bedrock, so the AWS credits pay for it. Paste the Bedrock key here once (from the AWS console: Bedrock, API keys). It is kept on the website and never shown again here. The voice uses the Azure key the site already has, and the Anthropic key the site already has is the backup. Nothing else to paste.

Apple Music

For the music player below. Three things from your Apple developer account: the Team ID, the Key ID, and the private key file (the .p8). Open the .p8 in a text editor and paste all of it. They are kept on the website and never shown again here. Not shown on screen: the listener signs in with their own Apple Music subscription, so ETL pays nothing for the music.

Spotify

For the music player. Make a free app at developer.spotify.com, then paste its Client ID here. The Client ID is public and not a secret, and no Spotify secret is used or kept anywhere. In the Spotify app's settings, add this exact address as a Redirect URI: . The listener signs in with their own Spotify Premium account.
