# Brief for ETL Design — ETL YOU

Paste the block below into ETL Design. Yuki anchors brand direction there,
so it is written for her to pick up. Everything after the block is context for
you, not for the brief.

## The brief

ETL YOU is Emerging Technologies Laboratory's youth division. Its first product,
Everly Castle, is live: ten princesses who teach real subjects to children aged
roughly three to six, in ten real countries, in ten languages.

A first brand sheet exists, generated by Gemini. Treat it as a starting position,
not a finished system. It proposes: an ETL YOU lockup built on ETL's three-bar
mark with YOU set small and raised; a palette of Spark Yellow, Teal Sea, Coral
Reef, Everly Indigo and a near-black; Spectral for display and Public Sans for
body; and a separate Everly Castle mark with a castle and a banner reading
EXPLORE. LEARN. PLAY.

What ETL YOU has to hold at once, and this is the hard part of the job:

The child cannot read. Every navigational decision she makes is made from a
picture. The identity has to work at 34px on a phone, in a circle, next to
painterly storybook artwork, with no text doing any work at all.

The parent must take it seriously. The same identity has to sit on a page that
says this teaches botany, geology and astronomy, next to ETL's professional
platforms, without looking like a toy brand that wandered in.

It is ETL, not a spin-off. It sits beside The Gauntlet, SLR Studio, Greylander
Press and the rest. Someone who knows ETL should recognise the parentage
immediately.

Deliver: the ETL YOU lockup in light and dark; the palette with the roles each
colour plays and the contrast pairs that are safe for small text; type scale for
display and body; the Everly Castle sub-mark; and a rule for how future ETL YOU
products relate to the parent mark.

Two constraints from the build, which are not preferences:

Any mark used in-product must be pure geometry and type, no raster. The castle
crest, the ten child badges and the ETL YOU lockup are all inline SVG so that
nothing has to load for identity to render, and nothing can go missing.

The product ships six child-chosen colour themes, so the identity must survive
being placed on any of them: Butterfly Blue, Mermaid Green, Sunshine Yellow,
Petal Pink, Snowflake White and Starry Night. Starry Night is a dark theme.

## Context for you, not for the brief

**What is already built and would have to be redone if the answer changes.**

The castle currently runs on ETL's existing palette rather than the four ETL YOU
colours: stamp blue `#28527a`, ink `#16202b`, ETL gold `#c9a23f`, blue tint
`#7fa8c9`. That was deliberate. Swapping to Spark Yellow and the rest is one
clean change to a variables block in two files, but doing it twice is waste, so
it is worth waiting for Yuki rather than guessing at the Gemini sheet.

The lockup in the product is already rebuilt on the three-bar geometry, so
unless Yuki changes the construction, that part is done.

Spectral and Public Sans are loading on both castle pages.

**What is wrong with the Gemini sheet, so you can discount it.**

The specimen text is nonsense. "The Dragon's paregory as a sample panssorp, and
heaut to finid to builsit on the first equario" is not language, and the caption
"Newly-designed mark tor a t!ase 'cloth'" is not either. That is generated
filler, and none of it should reach Yuki as though it were copy.

The activity cards at the bottom right imply a physical or printed product line
that does not exist. The URLs on the sheet (etlyou.com, everlycastle.org,
etlyou.shop) are invented and none are registered as far as this build knows.

**One question only you can answer.** The sheet gives Everly Castle its own mark
and tagline, separate from ETL YOU. That is a real decision with a cost: two
identities to maintain per product, and a second thing for a parent to learn. If
ETL YOU is going to have several products, a system where each gets a sub-mark
is worth building properly now. If Everly Castle is the whole of ETL YOU for the
foreseeable future, one identity is cheaper and clearer. Yuki should be told
which, because it changes what she designs.
