1 · Your PA
The first face you see

Your Personal Assistant runs your week. Every morning at 6, they record you a brief in their own voice: your inbox, your mentions, your calendar, your field. You talk to them all day; they dispatch the rest of the staff and report back. You choose your PA from twelve personas, and they are included in the base price.

4 · What else is in the room
Working features, day one
Your calendar, their hands

Paste your Outlook or Google calendar link once and your PA reads your real week: the morning brief flags collisions, countdowns, and the day you forgot you double-booked.

A desktop app

Install the Studio from your browser and it lives on your desktop with its own icon, like any app. No URL to remember.

Backpacks

Staff with backpacks carry live tools: web research, SEO scans, evidence lookups. Same skills as a chat bot, different reach.

Swap anybody

Your PA persona, your six-pack seats, your specialists: all swappable in the catalog. Firing is allowed. They do not take it personally.
