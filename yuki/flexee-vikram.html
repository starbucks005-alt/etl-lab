The italic is the weight of consequence. Amber reads as something at stake. Navy reads as enterprise software. The current site is navy.

The simulation cards

Flexee has two states for every discipline: what you chose, and what happened. The marketing site should show both. This is not a feature list. It is the product.

The decision

What happened
Margin  -12.4%
Late sourcing triggered a two-week stockout. Customers switched suppliers.
Utilization  87%
Staffing held. Discharge timing improved patient flow by 18%.
Runway  6 weeks
R&D allocation cut liquidity. Product shipped. Runway did not survive it.

A student looks left and remembers the choice. She looks right and sees what it cost. That is Flexee. The site should do that on the first scroll.

What I would not touch

The tagline. The simulation list. The pricing logic. The product is not the problem. The container is.

Type

Spectral for display. Public Sans for body. IBM Plex Mono for outcome data only. The mono is important: numbers that look like data feel more real than numbers that look like marketing.
